Surgical animations

Schematic surgical animations of the Lugones procedure and its anatomical variants — developed for educational use and surgical training.

Original technique
The Pericardial Tunnel
The scimitar vein is sutured to the pericardium to create a wide-lumen, tension-free tunnel directing blood into the left atrium.
Variant A
Atrial Septum Repositioning
For cases where the left atrium is small or the scimitar vein inserts high. The septum is repositioned to enlarge left atrial volume.
Variant B
Interatrial Pericardial Flap
An interatrial pericardial flap bridges the connection when standard tunneling is insufficient due to anatomical constraints.
